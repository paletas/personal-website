Bolinha Interactiva plays songs from the Bolinha da Música channel and adds interactive animations, synced to the music, for toddlers to tap along. I made it for my niece and nephew. The videos play through the official YouTube player, so the channel keeps its views and credit; the songs and videos are theirs.
