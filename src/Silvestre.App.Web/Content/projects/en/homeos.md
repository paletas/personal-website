HomeOS is a small self-hosted web app that gathers the everyday running of a household in one place. Recipes, the pantry, a weekly meal plan and the shopping list share one ingredient catalogue, so a recipe's ingredients go onto the list in one click, and it works in English and Portuguese. It keeps its data at home and is small enough to understand end to end.
